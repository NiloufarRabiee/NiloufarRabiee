# Research Vocabulary

## Niloufar Rabiee

This vocabulary is a working conceptual layer across my architecture, technology, and human–AI research. Definitions may evolve as projects produce stronger evidence.

### Perception
The process through which a system or person acquires signals from an environment. In my work, perception is deliberately separated from interpretation: observing behavior does not automatically justify claims about internal state.

### Presence
The felt continuity of an entity, interface, or intelligence across an interaction. Presence can be spatial, conversational, computational, or social.

### Agency
The person's ability to understand, interrupt, reject, redirect, or leave a system. A system is not meaningfully human-centered if the human cannot meaningfully alter the interaction.

### Adaptation
A change in system behavior in response to context. Adaptation should be bounded, legible, reversible when possible, and proportional to evidence.

### Continuity
The preservation of meaningful structure across time, sessions, transformations, or environments.

### Meaning
The significance a signal acquires for an experiencing person or within a specific context. Meaning is not treated as interchangeable with raw information.

### Restraint
The deliberate decision not to maximize intervention, automation, visibility, or certainty. In many systems, the correct response is smaller than the technically possible response.

### Spatial Intelligence
The ability of a designed environment or computational system to account for position, movement, orientation, context, and relationships in space without reducing space to a simple background variable.

This vocabulary is not presented as a finished theory. It is a compact research instrument for comparing ideas across disciplines.
