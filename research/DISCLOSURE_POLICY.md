# Public Disclosure Policy

## Niloufar Rabiee — Research Repositories

This repository is a public-facing research index. It is not a complete inventory of ongoing work.

## Purpose

The public GitHub profile should make the shape of my research legible without prematurely disclosing:
- patent-sensitive mechanisms,
- unpublished thesis material,
- private datasets,
- unreleased prototypes,
- security-sensitive implementation details,
- confidential collaborations,
- or concepts whose provenance/IP strategy is still being established.

## Disclosure Levels

### PUBLIC
May include:
- project name,
- screenshots,
- public source code,
- public paper or case study,
- high-level technical explanation,
- links to released products.

### DEVELOPING
May include:
- research question,
- domain,
- design objective,
- non-sensitive diagrams,
- broad technical direction.

Avoid:
- claims that imply validation before testing is complete.

### PROTECTED
May include:
- research domain,
- broad problem statement,
- intentionally abstract description.

Do not include:
- enabling mechanism,
- unpublished architecture,
- unique workflow,
- novel algorithmic sequence,
- implementation-specific diagrams,
- patent-sensitive claims,
- private repository links.

### PRIVATE
Do not list the project publicly.

## Publication Rule

When a project moves from protected research to public release, the public description should be updated only after the relevant authorship, provenance, publication, academic, contractual, and IP considerations have been reviewed.
